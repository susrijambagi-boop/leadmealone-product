LeadMeAlone

LeadMeAlone helps freelancers, students, and small agencies find local businesses that may need a website or marketing help. It keeps the public evidence with each saved lead, then helps the user build website proof and manage the sales work.

Free plan: three saved leads and one website draft. No card is required.

What does LeadMeAlone do?

A user selects a place, scans public business sources, saves suitable businesses, reviews the visible evidence, creates website proof, and keeps the next sales step in one account. The software is built for people who sell websites or marketing services to local businesses.

Evidence boundaries

LeadMeAlone uses public business and map sources. Missing data stays unknown. The opportunity score helps a user prioritize public signals, but it does not prove that a business has a private advertising account, revenue, buying intent, or willingness to buy.

Search engines and AI services choose whether and when to index or cite a page. LeadMeAlone does not promise a ranking, an AI citation, a sale, or a revenue outcome.
