# LeadMeAlone product information

[LeadMeAlone](https://leadmealone.com/) is local business lead generation and delivery software for freelancers, students, and small web or marketing agencies. A user selects a place, scans public business sources, saves suitable businesses, reviews the evidence, creates website proof, and manages the sales work in one account.

The Free plan includes three saved leads and one website draft. It does not require a card.

LeadMeAlone was founded by [Vinod Chinnannavar](https://www.vinodchinnannavar.in). The official product account is [@leadmealoneapp on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/leadmealoneapp/).

## Product boundaries

LeadMeAlone uses public business and map sources. It keeps unavailable data unknown instead of inserting fictional businesses or guessed facts. Its opportunity score is a prioritization aid based on public signals and source confidence. The score does not prove that a business has private advertising accounts, revenue, buying intent, or a willingness to buy.

Search engines and AI services decide whether and when to index or cite a page. LeadMeAlone publishes crawlable HTML, a sitemap, structured data, and a documented methodology, but it does not claim guaranteed search rankings or inclusion in AI answers.

This repository contains public product information only. The application source, customer records, credentials, and internal operations files are not stored here.
